# Introduction
RoboMaster is a robotics competition that challenges students to design and build robots that can compete in a variety of tasks, such as shooting projectiles, capturing flags, and navigating obstacle courses. The competition is a great way for students to apply their engineering skills in a fun and competitive environment, and it provides valuable hands-on experience in robotics, computer vision, and artificial intelligence.
