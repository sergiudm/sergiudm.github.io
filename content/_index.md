I'm a first-year PhD student at UC Santa Cruz in the [VLAA Lab](https://ucsc-vlaa.github.io/), advised by Prof. [Cihang Xie](https://cihangxie.github.io/). I build AI agents that operate real software: they read the screen, choose the next action, and, just as importantly, know when a task is actually done.

My recent work, [**VLAA-GUI**](/publications/#vlaa-gui), is a modular framework that teaches GUI agents when to *stop*, *recover*, and *search*. It reaches 77.5% on OSWorld and 61.0% on WindowsAgentArena. More broadly, I'm interested in multimodal language models, computer-use agents, and visual representation learning. Here you'll find my publications and a [blog](/blog/).
